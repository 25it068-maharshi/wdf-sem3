# StudentHub Requirements

## Roles
- Student: register/login, dashboard, attendance, timetable, study material, assignments, notices, profile, contact faculty.
- Faculty: login, upload material, create assignments, view submissions, mark attendance, post notices.
- Admin: manage students, faculty, courses, notices, timetable and accounts.

## Core pages
Home, Login, Registration, Dashboard, Attendance, Assignments, Study Material, Timetable, Notice Board, Profile, Contact Us, Admin Panel.
